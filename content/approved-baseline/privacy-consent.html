Privacy & Consent

Information used only for tutoring and related administration.

Auxesis Education collects only the information needed to respond to an enquiry, arrange tutoring and manage the tutoring relationship. Depending on the situation, this may include a parent or guardian’s name, email address and phone number; the student’s first name and personal Gmail address; country, optional nationality and pronouns, and time zone; grade or year level; curriculum or programme; subject; learning goals or challenges; scheduling information; and whether tutoring is being requested for more than one student. During tutoring, this also includes lesson planning, lesson notes, resource links, messages, progress reports, invoices and payment records held in the private Auxesis Portal.

Student phone numbers are not normally collected or used. Students communicate with me through their private message thread in the Auxesis Portal. Parents may choose to create a WhatsApp group after enrolment, and I am happy to participate, but WhatsApp is not a required student communication channel.

Personal information is used only for tutoring and related administration. It is not added to a marketing mailing list, sold or shared for marketing. Information is kept only for as long as needed for tutoring and the administration connected with it.

Auxesis Education does not record tutoring sessions. Students may choose to record their own sessions, but those recordings are for their personal educational use only. The Auxesis Education website does not currently use analytics or advertising trackers.

The Auxesis Education website and live private Portal run on Cloudflare Pages and Workers. Cloudflare D1 stores Portal accounts, lesson records, messages, progress reports, invoices and payment records, as well as submitted reviews and their consent and moderation status. This describes record storage; it does not mean Cloudflare collects payments or stores every document used for tutoring. The live Portal no longer runs on Floot.

Google provides Portal sign-in and processes the account information used to identify authorised users. Google Meet is used for live tutoring sessions. Google Calendar holds scheduling information, and the Google Sheets Student Tracker remains the authoritative billing record. Google Drive is used for shared lesson documents and privately stored recovery packages; these files are separate from the Cloudflare Portal database.

Reviews submitted through the website are stored in Cloudflare D1. Your comment, chosen public name or description, any optional star rating and permission to publish are recorded. A review remains private until I approve it; only approved reviews with publication consent are displayed publicly. The review service does not email submissions. It uses a hidden spam field, a one-use form check and submission limits, including an IP-derived identifier for rate limiting.

Tutoring enquiries submitted through this website are checked using Cloudflare Turnstile and sent through Resend to Amanda’s Gmail inbox, which is handled by Google. The enquiry includes the contact and learning-support details you provide and your consent to be contacted. Resend processes the message and delivery information to provide this service. You can also email the contact address shown on this website directly.

The Portal uses necessary sign-in and session cookies. Cloudflare processes technical request information to deliver the website and its services, and Google processes information needed for its sign-in and other services. These functions are separate from advertising or marketing tracking.

GitHub Actions is used for controlled deployment and migration workflows, which can temporarily process recovery data. GitHub is not the live Portal database, and private production records are not published in the public website repository.
